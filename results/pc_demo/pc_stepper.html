PC Algorithm Stepper

Generate data from a structure you know, then watch the PC algorithm rebuild it from independence tests on that data, one test at a time.

  1. Start with every pair of columns linked
  2. Level 0: remove pairs that are independent
  3. Level 1, 2, …: remove pairs that become independent given 1, 2, … neighbours, and remember what separated them
  4. Colliders: X – Z – Y with X, Y unlinked and Z not in their separating set becomes X → Z ← Y
  5. Propagate directions that avoid new colliders and cycles
